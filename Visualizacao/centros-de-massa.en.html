Atlas of Centres of Mass (1985 → 2024)

A spatial comparison of the 19 centroids computed in the study. Each point is the weighted centre of gravity of one variable over the 166 AMCs of Goiás. While the agropastoral frontier marches north, dairy cattle, the urban area, rural credit and silos stay anchored in the centre-south — and soy shows why the MapBiomas raster and SIDRA planted area diverge in Act III.
